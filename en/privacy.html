Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026 · Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy explains how your personal data is processed in the MULE mobile app. Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), the data controller is Eren Akyıldız, who publishes the app. Contact: info@aina.love.

1. What does MULE do?

MULE is not a cargo or transport service. It is a matching platform that brings together a driver who is already making a planned trip and the owner of an item heading the same way. The carriage relationship, the payment and responsibility for the item are entirely between the users.

3. Data we do not process

  • Credit card, bank account and payment details are not processed. Payment happens outside the app, between the parties.
  • No advertising identifier is collected; no data is shared with ad networks.
  • Your behaviour outside the app is not tracked and is not matched with other apps.
  • Personal data is not sold and is not transferred to third parties for marketing.

4. Location data

Location is taken only while the app is open and only if you have given permission; no location is collected in the background. If you refuse permission you can keep using the app; only the "near me" feature on the map will not work. During an active trip the item owner sees the travel companion's location rounded to about 500 metres; the exact location is not shared. Sharing starts with the match and ends when the hand-over is confirmed.

5. Photos

Item photos are used only to show what the item is and to serve as evidence in a hand-over dispute. Only the person you matched with can see them. They are deleted 90 days after the listing closes.

6. Storage and location

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) region. Account records are kept for as long as your account is open; listing and hand-over records for at most 2 years, taking the limitation period for disputes into account; messages for 1 year. At the end of these periods the data is deleted or anonymised.

7. Transfers

Data is transferred only to the infrastructure providers needed for the service to work: Supabase (database and file storage, EU), Apple (app distribution and crash reports). Lawful requests from authorised public authorities are reserved.

8. Your rights (KVKK art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information, to ask for it to be corrected, deleted or destroyed, to ask for processing to be restricted, to obtain a copy of your data and to object to processing. You can send your requests to info@aina.love.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in the app, with Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion permanently removes your profile, your listings, your messages and your photos; it cannot be undone. Records of completed hand-overs, to which the other side is also a party, may be kept with your identity stripped out. You can also ask for deletion by email.

10. Children

MULE is not designed for people under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page is updated; significant changes are announced in the app.
