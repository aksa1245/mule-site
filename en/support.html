Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and needs no e-mail. Your listings, messages and photos are deleted with it; records tied to a completed hand-over may be kept where the law requires.

Reporting a listing or a person

Use the "…" menu on an item's owner row, or the "…" menu in a chat. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also write to the address above.

What MULE is not

MULE is not a transport, courier, parcel or postal service. It puts a driver already on the road in touch with someone whose item is heading the same way. The arrangement, the cost contribution and the payment are directly between users; MULE is not a party to them and takes no cut.
