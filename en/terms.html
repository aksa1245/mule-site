Terms of Use

Effective date: 17 September 2026

1. What MULE is and is not

MULE is a matching platform that brings together drivers who have already planned a trip and people whose item is heading the same way. MULE does not provide a transport, cargo, courier or postal service; it is not a party to the carriage, it does not enter into a contract of carriage and it does not undertake that the item will be carried. The contract is formed directly between the item owner and the travel companion.

2. Cost contribution

The amount in the app is not a carriage fee but a contribution to the cost of the trip. The contributions collected on a trip cannot exceed that trip's fuel and toll cost; on every trip the driver covers at least a quarter of their own cost themselves. This limit is enforced by the app. The driver is not meant to make a profit and is not permitted to.

3. Payment

MULE currently takes no fee or commission and does not come between the payment. The contribution is paid between the parties at the moment of hand-over. MULE is not responsible for the payment, for non-payment or for a disagreement over the amount.

4. The user's obligations

  • Being over 18 and giving correct information.
  • Item owner: declaring the contents of the item correctly, uploading a photo that clearly shows the contents, closing the parcel in front of the travel companion.
  • Travel companion: making their own planned trip, with a valid driving licence and a vehicle that has compulsory motor insurance.
  • Both sides: keeping to the meeting and hand-over times, treating the other side with respect.

5. Prohibited contents

Unlawful items of any kind; and also cash and negotiable instruments, prescription medicine, alcohol and tobacco products, weapons and their parts, explosive, flammable and corrosive substances, chemicals, live animals, perishable food and sealed parcels whose contents are hidden may not be carried. Sales of commercial goods and e-commerce parcels are outside the scope of the platform.

6. Liability

Liability for the loss of, damage to or delay of the item lies between the parties and is subject to the general provisions of law. MULE is not responsible for harm users cause one another, for the accuracy of a listing's contents or for a meeting that does not take place. Compulsory motor insurance does not cover a third party's item carried in the vehicle; separate insurance is recommended for valuable items.

7. Suspension of an account

Accounts that breach these terms, have prohibited items carried, give false information, harass others or carry items regularly for profit may be suspended or closed without warning.

8. Reporting content

Every listing and every user in the app has report and block options. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours; unlawful content is removed.

9. Changes and governing law

If these terms change, this page is updated and significant changes are announced in the app. These terms are governed by the law of the Republic of Türkiye.
