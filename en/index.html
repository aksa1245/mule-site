MULE

The empty space in your car, and the item on your road.

MULE brings together a driver already on the road and an item heading the same way. It is not a courier service: the trip is happening anyway, and the cost of the road is shared. The driver makes no profit — on every trip they cover at least a quarter of their own cost out of their own pocket, and the contributions collected can never exceed the cost of the trip.

How it works

  • Share the route you are taking anyway and the free space in your car.
  • See the items along it, and what each one adds in kilometres, minutes and cost.
  • Agree the cost contribution; it is worked out from the day's real pump price.
  • Meet along the route or at the address; a single-use code is read at hand-over.

MULE takes no cut. No commission, no service charge. You agree the contribution between yourselves and settle it between yourselves at hand-over.
